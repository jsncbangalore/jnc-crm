Functional Specification

Comprehensive Enterprise Operations Capabilities

Every module is built with unified business logic, role-based access control, and strict schema validation.

Document Pipeline & Invoicing

Seamless document conversion from Approved Quotation to Confirmed Order to Tax Invoice, SEZ Invoice, Delivery Challan, or Credit Note. Document numbering strictly respects organizational prefixes.

Single Payment Engine

All financial payments route through a single verified payment service. Allocations support specific invoice settling or FIFO auto-allocation with double-entry general ledger tracking.

Granular Role Permissions

Configurable page and action permissions across Sales Executives, Sales Managers, Inventory Clerks, Finance Accountants, and Tenant Administrators.

Audit Logging & Compliance

Every financial posting, document conversion, and security configuration event is permanently immutably logged with timestamp and user ID.
