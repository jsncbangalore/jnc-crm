Information Governance

Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Apex Systems Technologies Ltd. is committed to safeguarding customer data, confidential business documents, and customer access records.

1. Information Collection

We process only account credentials, tenant configurations, and user transactions explicitly uploaded to the software instance.

2. Encryption Standards

All sensitive credentials and API tokens are secured with AES-256-GCM symmetric encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

3. Access Control

Tenant data isolation is enforced through multi-tenant database partitioning. Staff access is strictly governed by zero-trust identity policies.

4. Contact Information

For data privacy queries or erasure requests, contact contact@example.com.
